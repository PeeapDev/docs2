# Pricing & Economics

Peeap is built to help you **grow your platform**. The rails your users touch most
— collecting fees, moving money between wallets, reading balances — are **free**,
forever. We make our money at the edges of the wallet, not on the payments your
users make inside it.

<Note>
There is **no integration fee, no monthly minimum, and no charge to collect
money**. If you never move money *out* to a bank, you never pay us.
</Note>

## What's free

Everything your users do inside the Peeap wallet:

| Capability | Cost |
|-----------|------|
| Collect school fees / payments ([initiate](/api-reference/schools/payments) → authorize) | **Free** |
| Wallet-to-wallet transfers | **Free** |
| Read [balance](/api-reference/wallet/balance) & [transactions](/api-reference/wallet/transactions) | **Free** |
| [Checkout](/api-reference/checkout/create) sessions & USSD codes | **Free** |
| [Webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks/manage) | **Free** |
| Sign in with Peeap ([OAuth](/api-reference/auth/oauth)) | **Free** |

This is deliberate. The more of your users hold a Peeap balance, the stronger the
whole network — so we don't tax the thing that grows it.

## What we charge for

Only when money **enters** or **leaves** the wallet ecosystem, or converts into a
product — never on the payments in between:

| Service | Who pays | Model |
|---------|----------|-------|
| **Cash-in** (top-up from mobile money) | The end user (same rate they'd pay any agent) | A spread over the network cost — invisible on your bill |
| **Cash-out** ([payout](/api-reference/wallet/payouts) to bank / momo) | The end user withdrawing | A spread over the network cost |
| **Card issuance** ([purchase a card](/api-reference/cards/purchase)) | The end user buying the card | Flat price per card |

Your users pay these at the point they'd pay a fee anyway — cashing out at an
agent, or buying a card. You never see a line item for collecting fees.

## What you earn — developer revenue share

You're bringing users onto the network, so you share in what they generate. Peeap
pays you a **share of the cash-out margin** on the users you onboard. The more your
platform grows real money movement, the more you earn — automatically, settled to
your merchant account.

<Info>
Rev-share is set per partner. It turns Peeap from "a payment SDK you pay for" into
"a rail that pays you back for growth." Talk to us to set your rate.
</Info>

## The network effect

A Peeap wallet you create for a user isn't locked to your app. It works across
**every** Peeap-powered platform — your users become reachable by the whole
ecosystem, and users from other apps can transact in yours. You're not just
integrating a payment method; you're plugging into a shared wallet graph.

## In one line

> Collecting money is free. We earn a thin spread when money enters or leaves the
> wallet, and on cards — and we share the cash-out margin back with you for the
> users you bring.
