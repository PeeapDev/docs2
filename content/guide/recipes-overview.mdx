# Recipes

Parts 1–3 taught you the building blocks. This part puts them together into
**complete, working projects** — one per common use case. Each recipe is
self-contained: copy it, swap in your keys, and adapt.

Every recipe follows the same proven spine you already know — create a session,
send the customer to pay, **confirm server-side, fulfil idempotently, reconcile** —
so they reinforce the habits from Part 3 rather than inventing new ones.
